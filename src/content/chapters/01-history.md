## However, they were wrong!

**Plasma had other ideas** — The axisymmetric Z-pinch,
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \vec{u} &= u_{z}(r)\hat{e}_{z} \\
    \vec{B} &= B_{\theta}(r)\hat{e}_{\theta}
\end{aligned}
$$
may have been one of the simplest possible magnetic confinement fusion configurations (MCF) that promised an economical approach to the task without needing applied magnetic fields, but it was prey to virulent fundamental magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) modes that limited lifetimes to nanoseconds (ns) -- too short for fusion to create net power.
