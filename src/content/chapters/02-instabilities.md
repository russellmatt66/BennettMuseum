## The Sausage and Kink modes

**Two Positive Feedback Loops** — When studying plasma instabilities in the Z-pinch we consider perturbations to the plasma displacement whose azimuthal, $\theta$, and axial, $z$ components are described by a plane-wave,
$$
\xi \propto \exp\bigg(i (m \theta + k z - \omega t)\bigg)
$$
The sausage mode, $m = 0$, and kink mode, $m = 1$, are the two primary instabilities that plague the Z-pinch. Ways to stabilize the system against this either require means that are incompatible with a fusion reactor, e.g., wall-stabilization, or introduce externally applied magnetic fields and do away with the main advantage of the Z-pinch: it generates its own confinement.
